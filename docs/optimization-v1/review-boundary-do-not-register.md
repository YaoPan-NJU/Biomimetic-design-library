# Boundary And DO-NOT Register

status: active

## Rules

- `hard_do_not` requires direct literature support and must include a locator/quote.
- `soft_boundary` requires literature support or clear review support and should not block by itself.
- `knowledge_gap` is allowed for important missing/inferred boundaries and must not be treated as a hard rule.
- No boundary is written to `prototypes_db` until Yao approves it.
