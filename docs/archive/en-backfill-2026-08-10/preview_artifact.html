oat4-organic-anion-transporter

3 mechanisms
organism Homo sapiens OAT4 有机阴离子转运体 → Homo sapiens OAT4 organic anion transporter
name
阴离子头基与芳香-阳离子底物口袋的两点位识别（硫酸结合型有机阴离子）
Two-point recognition of anionic head group by an aromatic-cationic substrate pocket (sulfate-conjugated organic anions)
description
人 OAT4（SLC22A11，550 氨基酸残基、12 跨膜域）表达于肾近端小管顶端膜与胎盘，以高亲和力、钠离子非依赖方式转运硫酸结合型有机阴离子：雌酮硫酸酯 E1S（Km 1.01 µM）与硫酸脱氢表雄酮 DHEAS（Km 0.63 µM），并转运赭曲霉毒素 A（Cha 2000）。人 OAT4·DHEAS 冷冻电镜结构 PDB 9U5A（2025-06-18 释放）坐标直测：配体 ZWY（DHEAS，17-氧代雄甾-5-烯-3β-基硫酸氢酯）4.0 Å 内含芳香残基 Phe211、Phe238、Tyr360、Tyr361、Met231 与阳离子 Arg389、极性 Gln242（chain A，PDB 编号）；该芳香笼加阳离子锚为 SLC22 家族保守底物结合基序（大鼠 OAT1 PDB 8SDY 同源直测：Tyr230/Phe438/Tyr353/Phe443 芳香笼 + Lys382/Arg466 阳离子残基，Dou 2023）
Human OAT4 (SLC22A11; 550 amino acid residues, 12 transmembrane domains), expressed on the apical membrane of renal proximal tubule cells and in placenta, transports sulfate-conjugated organic anions in a high-affinity, sodium-independent manner: estrone sulfate E1S (Km 1.01 uM) and dehydroepiandrosterone sulfate DHEAS (Km 0.63 uM), and also transports ochratoxin A (Cha 2000). In the human OAT4-DHEAS cryo-EM structure (PDB 9U5A, released 2025-06-18), the ligand ZWY (DHEAS, 17-oxoandrost-5-en-3beta-yl hydrogen sulfate) is within 4.0 A of the aromatic residues Phe211, Phe238, Tyr360, Tyr361, Met231, the cation Arg389, and the polar residue Gln242 (chain A, PDB numbering). This aromatic cage plus cationic anchor constitutes a conserved substrate-binding motif of the SLC22 family (rat OAT1 PDB 8SDY homologous measurement: aromatic cage Tyr230/Phe438/Tyr353/Phe443 plus cationic residues Lys382/Arg466, Dou 2023).
transferable principle
阳离子/极性头基锚加芳香-疏水骨架笼的两点位识别构型，可以钠离子非依赖方式识别化学结构多样的阴离子分子；头基化学（硫酸结合物优先于葡萄糖醛酸结合物）与阴离子电荷是选择性的关键决定因素
A two-point recognition configuration of a cationic/polar head-group anchor plus an aromatic-hydrophobic scaffold cage can recognize chemically diverse anionic molecules in a sodium-independent manner; head-group chemistry (sulfate conjugates preferred over glucuronide conjugates) and anionic charge are key determinants of selectivity.

beta-cyclodextrin-hostguest-inclusion

1 mechanisms
organism 环糊精：直链淀粉（植物多糖，α-D-吡喃葡萄糖 α-1,4 连接）经细菌 CGTase 酶促环化的天然环状寡糖；β-CD 为 7 葡萄糖单元大环，具疏水内腔、亲水外缘 → Cyclodextrin: a naturally occurring cyclic oligosaccharide formed by enzymatic cyclization of amylose (a plant polysaccharide of α-D-glucopyranose units linked α-1,4) by bacterial CGTase; β-CD is a macrocycle of 7 glucose units with a hydrophobic inner cavity and a hydrophilic outer rim
name
β-环糊精疏水内腔的主客体包结识别
Host-guest inclusion recognition by the hydrophobic inner cavity of β-cyclodextrin
description
β-环糊精由 7 个 α-D-葡萄糖以 α-1,4 连接成锥筒，外缘羟基亲水、内腔疏水；水相中经疏水效应与范德华/尺寸互补将芳香或疏水客体（如双酚 A、氯酚、多环麝香、紫外过滤剂等）包结进内腔形成 1:1（或 2:1）主客体复合物，客体自体相水去溶剂进入低极性腔是主要驱动力。
β-Cyclodextrin is a truncated cone formed by 7 α-D-glucose units linked via α-1,4 bonds, with a hydrophilic outer rim of hydroxyls and a hydrophobic inner cavity. In the aqueous phase, aromatic or hydrophobic guests (e.g., bisphenol A, chlorophenols, polycyclic musks, UV filters) are included into the cavity through hydrophobic effects and van der Waals/size complementarity, forming 1:1 (or 2:1) host-guest complexes; desolvation of the guest from bulk water into the low-polarity cavity is the main driving force.
transferable principle
两亲环状寡糖以疏水内腔 + 尺寸/形状互补包结疏水芳香客体；交联为多孔聚合物即得快速、可再生、耐基质的广谱微污染物吸附材料；选择性上限受 1:1 容量与广谱疏水包结约束。
An amphiphilic cyclic oligosaccharide includes hydrophobic aromatic guests via its hydrophobic cavity plus size/shape complementarity; crosslinking it into a porous polymer yields a fast, regenerable, matrix-tolerant broad-spectrum adsorption material for micropollutants; the selectivity ceiling is constrained by 1:1 capacity and broad-spectrum hydrophobic inclusion.
