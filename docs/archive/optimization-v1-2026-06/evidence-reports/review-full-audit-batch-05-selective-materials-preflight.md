## Audit Summary

- `dna-aptamer` is effectively underbuilt: no performance rows, one mechanism, zero provenance, and an empty enrichment JSON. Local PDFs and extraction JSONs exist for aptamer reviews, biosensors, heavy-metal/cadmium water-treatment, microcystin, pathogens, and one 2026 aptamer-aflatoxin patent, so this should become a targeted evidence-build sub-batch.
- `diatom-frustule` has many real local PDFs, but source paths in JSON often omit the actual directory or ` 2.pdf` suffix. Several row groups are duplicated, especially Abou-Elanwar2025 and Sriram2022 rows, and quote/locator fields are blank.
- `mangrove-root` is system-level constructed-wetland evidence, not a conventional adsorbent material. Its performance rows are removal percentages or blank rows for NH4+-N/PO4 removal; they must not be compared directly to adsorption qmax.
- `alginate`, `cellulose-nanocrystal`, `metal-organic-framework`, and `starch-granule` are material-reference stores, not ordinary biological prototypes. They contain hundreds of review-derived numeric rows and should be audited by material/source family before ranking or JSON cleanup.
- `metal-organic-framework` is especially risky because `provenance_summary.n_verified = 252`, while many rows use `verification = single_source`. In this audit protocol, `single_source` is not equivalent to fully PDF-verified quote+locator evidence.
